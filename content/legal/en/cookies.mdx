This policy explains how **whfdev.com** uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, mobile phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and analyze how it is used.

## Cookies used on this site

### Strictly necessary cookies

These work without consent as they are essential for basic site functionality:

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remember the chosen language (pt-BR, pt-PT, en, fr) | 1 year |

### Analytics cookies

Allow us to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Data is anonymized and aggregated.

| Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Google Analytics** (G-B4F22YPY1E) | Anonymous traffic stats, popular pages, traffic sources | up to 2 years |

We do not use advertising, retargeting or behavioral profiling cookies.

## Managing cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies at any time. Most browsers allow you to:

- See which cookies are stored
- Delete cookies individually or all at once
- Block cookies from specific sites
- Block third-party cookies

Instructions for the most-used browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari (macOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471)
- [Safari (iOS)](https://support.apple.com/HT201265)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/help/4027947)

Blocking cookies may affect your browsing experience.

## Opting out of Google Analytics

You can install the official [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to prevent the tool from collecting data on any site you visit.
