This policy explains how **whfdev.com** uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, mobile phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and analyze how it is used.

## Cookies used on this site

### Strictly necessary cookies

These work without consent as they are essential for basic site functionality:

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remember the chosen language (pt-BR, pt-PT, en, fr) | 1 year |
| `whfdev-consent` | Store your choice in the cookie banner (accept or decline) | 6 months |

### Analytics cookies (with consent)

Only switched on after you click “Accept all” in the cookie banner. Until then, Google Analytics runs in denied consent mode (Google Consent Mode): it sets no cookies and sends only aggregated, identifier-free signals.

| Provider | Cookies | Purpose | Duration |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|
| **Google Analytics** (G-B4F22YPY1E) | `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Traffic statistics, popular pages and traffic sources | up to 2 years |

We also use **Vercel Web Analytics**, which uses neither cookies nor persistent identifiers (see “Cookie-free statistics”).

### Advertising cookies (with consent)

Only switched on after you accept in the banner. They serve Google AdSense ads and measure their performance.

| Provider | Cookies | Purpose | Duration |
|----------|---------|---------|----------|
| **Google AdSense** | `__gads`, `__gpi`, `IDE`, `test_cookie` (google.com and doubleclick.net domains) | Ad serving and measurement | up to 13 months |

Learn how Google uses this data at [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and adjust your ad preferences at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

No analytics or advertising cookie is set before you consent, and declining does not limit your use of the site.

## Managing your consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the **“Manage cookies”** link in the site footer, which shows the banner again. When you decline, the Google Analytics cookies set by this site are deleted; advertising cookies on Google domains can only be removed in your browser settings (below) or at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Managing cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies at any time. Most browsers allow you to:

- See which cookies are stored
- Delete cookies individually or all at once
- Block cookies from specific sites
- Block third-party cookies

Instructions for the most-used browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari (macOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471)
- [Safari (iOS)](https://support.apple.com/HT201265)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/help/4027947)

Blocking cookies may affect your browsing experience.

## Cookie-free statistics

To understand which pages are visited most and which countries visits come from, we use [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy). It sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and does not track across sites: each visit is tied to a temporary hash that is discarded daily, and only aggregated data is kept. Processing is carried out by Vercel Inc. under a data processing agreement with EU standard contractual clauses. Because it uses no cookies or persistent identifiers, no prior consent is required.
