This Privacy Policy describes how **WHFDEV Consultoria em Tecnologia LTDA** (CNPJ 46.185.304/0001-71), hereinafter "WHFDEV", processes personal data collected through the whfdev.com website.

## Data controller

WHFDEV Consultoria em Tecnologia LTDA
CNPJ: 46.185.304/0001-71
Contact: [talkto@whfdev.com](mailto:talkto@whfdev.com)

## What data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily provide via the contact form:

- **Name**
- **Email address**
- **Company** (optional)
- **Project type** (CRM, dashboard, app, etc.)
- **Estimated budget**
- **Desired timeline**
- **Project description**

We also collect anonymous navigation data through Google Analytics (see Cookie Policy).

## Purpose of processing

Contact form data is used exclusively to:

1. Respond to your commercial inquiry
2. Maintain an internal sales history
3. Comply with legal obligations when applicable

We do not send newsletters, marketing communications or unsolicited promotional material.

## Legal basis

- **GDPR (EU):** consent (art. 6, 1, a) and legitimate interest (art. 6, 1, f) to respond to commercial inquiries.
- **LGPD (Brazil):** consent (art. 7, I) and legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).

## Sharing with third parties

To deliver our services, we share only strictly necessary data with:

- **Resend** ([resend.com](https://resend.com)) — sending the form email
- **Google Analytics** ([google.com/analytics](https://google.com/analytics)) — aggregated and anonymous metrics
- **Vercel** ([vercel.com](https://vercel.com)) — website hosting

These processors have their own policies and are accountable for their obligations under GDPR/LGPD.

## Retention

Contact form data is kept for **24 months** from the last interaction. After this period, it is deleted or anonymized.

## Your rights

At any time, you can exercise the rights provided by GDPR and LGPD:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate or outdated data
- Request deletion of your data
- Request data portability
- Withdraw consent
- Get information about who your data has been shared with
- File a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email [talkto@whfdev.com](mailto:talkto@whfdev.com). We reply within 15 business days.

## Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect your data: HTTPS communication, storage with certified providers (Vercel, Resend), and restricted access to the controller.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated at any time. The current version is always the one published at this address, with the update date indicated at the top.

## Contact

For questions about this policy or data processing, write to [talkto@whfdev.com](mailto:talkto@whfdev.com).
